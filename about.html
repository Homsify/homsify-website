About Homsify

Property care shouldn't take five phone calls.

We started Homsify because property owners and managers kept describing the same problem — a different vendor for every trade, no single point of accountability, and no real idea when something would actually get fixed.

Our Approach

One accountable crew, every trade covered.

Homsify runs a single maintenance operation across painting, plumbing, electrical, cleaning, renovations, handyman work, snow & ice, and tub reglazing. Every job — big or small — is scoped with a written quote, scheduled with a confirmed crew, and closed out with a clean invoice.

That means one phone number to remember, one team to trust, and no more chasing separate contractors for a single project.

  • Licensed & insured on every job we take on
  • Upfront, written quotes — no surprise line items
  • One point of contact from first call to closed ticket
  • Serving London & Southwestern Ontario
